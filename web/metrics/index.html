Map scaling changes only the visualization. Scores and downloaded raw values retain their metric definitions. Native similarity maps retain their metric sampling grids; visualization uses 1−similarity. VSI displays saliency-weighted quality contributions. FSIM/FSIMc maps show luminance similarity; MS-SSIM shows level-0 SSIM. Hatched cells mark uncovered borders or undefined native values; HaarPSI has undefined local similarity in zero-weight windows. NLPD, PSNR-HVS, MAD, VIF and VMAF maps show one native component of their score (NLPD level 0, PSNR-HVS per-block CSF error, MAD's high-quality strategy, VIF's finest information loss, VMAF's SPEED chroma-U blocks), not a per-pixel score; IW-SSIM shows scale-0 contrast-structure and mDCT-PSNR each window's detection probability. IW-SSIM maps need both sides of at least 176 pixels; VMAF v1 needs even dimensions, both sides of at least 33 and one of at least 216. SSIMULACRA2 shows its per-pixel terms fused with the score's own weights; the map's mean is the weighted sum the score transforms. Browser timings are interactive feedback, not accepted fleet benchmark observations.
