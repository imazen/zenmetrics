# Retained canonical runtime evidence

The selected PaQ2PiQ warm-eight-worker and DeepDC/LPIPS-VGG first-evaluation
cohorts do not retain independent foreign-CPU observations for their canonical
measurement windows. Source review and two completed blocks per cohort confirm
that the comparators did not invoke a contention observer. Whole-process GNU
time and outer resource-wrapper logs cannot reconstruct a call-local gate.
This is missing evidence; it does not demonstrate that interference occurred.

PaQ2PiQ's native warm arm retains its zenbench round flags. The two first-call
probes do not run that inner gate on either arm. Those first-call results must
retain their descriptive qualification rather than inherit the warm worker's
resource qualification. Existing numerical checks and retained score values
are unaffected.

The [source and bounded receipt index](runtime-cleanliness-evidence.json) binds
fourteen source files and six completed block samples with their named arm
outputs, memory receipts and wrapper logs. The coordinator independently
verified all 66 indexed hashes. This review covers three selected routes, not
all canonical arms or the complete benchmark matrix. It introduces no new
acceptance threshold, numerical bound or retrospective waiver.

A future observer would require a separately controlled successor: preserve
the scorer commands and clocks, record owner process identities and independent
system observations over the child lifetime, and treat missing coverage as
unqualified. No such observer or replacement timing run is implemented by this
source review.
