# Headless resident HDR successor

Missing qualification: nine fresh headless preflights, four warm photo512 ABBA
cases, corresponding resident-cell protocol parity and native parity/resource
controls. No scorer or Octave process is launched by the source preparer or its
static checker. Scope remains false and qualified timing cells remain zero.
V2 keeps 5e-3; the official v3 photo cohort still has no assigned native bound.

`first_boundary_hdrvdp_headless.py` copies the frozen resize2201 source parent
into a NEW folder. Original metric/observer/loader/resize source bytes are
preserved. Only wrapper paths relocate to those copies; every timed evaluator
body must remain byte-identical to the predecessor and its recorded hash. Every
control command adds `--no-window-system` alongside `--no-gui`. The original
104-case one/twenty-eight-thread source contract remains in the new parent for
provenance and compatibility with the unchanged preflight runner. The visible
successor queue selects only four warm cases: HDRv2/HDRv3 at one/eight threads,
16 original/resident ABBA arms. No all104 launch is prepared.

Nine new headless preflight results must pass before the new cohort can seal.
The old non-headless preflight receipt is not reused. Sealing requires its
explicit result SHA256 and validates the full logs/snapshots/artifact closure.
Each arm then acquires both shared locks and run-heavy admission independently.
The corrected worker audits retained results inside admission, including
terminal reuse; exact finite score bits and the complete raw closure are
required. No automatic retry or old-coordinator resume is performed.

The output `commands.json` contains three ordered root-owned operations:

1. `preflight`: one admitted driver for all nine fresh controls.
2. `seal`: supply the SHA256 of the passed headless preflight result.
3. `warm_four`: supply the SHA256 of that new passed seal. The coordinator
   acquires/releases admission separately per arm or retained audit.

The seal/queue commands must not receive an outer shared lock because they
acquire admission themselves. Each result/log path is new and refuses overwrite.
The retained source parent, old v2 queues, their valid observations and any
already-admitted old arm remain untouched.

The source-only checker prepares the bundle, checks original source copies and
timed bodies, validates the four selected cases with the actual worker context,
and repeats the six retained-audit controls. It does not establish numerical or
runtime acceptance. Root's separate X11 trace motivates the flag change; new
controls must establish its equivalence on these exact sources.

The resident cell already has both headless flags. After the fresh headless seal
exists, its `prepare` command can generate another NEW cell config and two 1/8
candidate plans against this parent/seal. Its photo512 body hashes are fixed to
the exact photo case, not another grid size. Such plans remain false-scoped until
their corresponding gates actually pass. Original AIC 200-nit XYZ routes remain
separate and unchanged.
