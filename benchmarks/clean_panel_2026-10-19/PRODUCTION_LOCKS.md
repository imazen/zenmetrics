checks device/inode and canonical paths, and acquires them in declared order.
Kernel fdinfo witnesses bind the wrapper PID/start identity to both flocks.
The monitor controller retains before/after witnesses and ancestor identities.
Cleanup waits on recorded PID/start identities; release evidence states only
that this owner unlocked, not that no subsequent owner holds the locks.

Owned fixture controls cover missing paths, directories, symlinks, mismatched
inodes, positive kernel ownership, reverse release ordering and unchanged file
bytes/inodes. These controls do not establish production admission. Session
sampling is bounded observational evidence, not proof of unseen escaped children.
